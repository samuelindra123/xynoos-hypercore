01 / From a signal to a decision

The interesting part
is the space between.

Automation is easy to promise. Knowing whether to intervene is a harder engineering problem.

01
OBSERVABILITY

See pressure in context.

PressureIQ qualifies Linux memory, CPU and I/O signals. Stale or unavailable inputs must change the decision, not silently disappear.

↗

EXPLAINABILITY
